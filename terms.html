Information about browsing this website and requesting rentals.

Using the showroom

The website helps you explore rentals, choose quantities and request a personalized quote. Submitting a request does not create a reservation or rental agreement.

Product information

Images may include styling inspiration or accessories that are not part of the rental item. Product descriptions identify the listed item. Availability, services and final pricing require confirmation by GNS.

Your rental agreement

The agreement and quote supplied for your event set the applicable booking, payment, cancellation, delivery, setup and item-care terms. Review those documents before accepting a booking.

Questions

Contact GNS about any website information or event requirements before making a booking. Please do not send payment card details through the quote form.
